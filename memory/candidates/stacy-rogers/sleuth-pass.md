It lists Governor/Lt. Governor as a 2026 office and gives the primary filing deadline as 12:00 p.m. Monday, June 1, 2026. ([Kansas SOS candidate information](https://www.sos.ks.gov/elections/candidate-information.html))

Smith" for Governor / Lieutenant Governor of Kansas, party Republican, incumbent No, office level State Offices, term four years. ## Issue-mapped online observations

### Issue: Government accountability, transparency, and anti-insider politics

- The campaign homepage frames Rogers's campaign around listening to Kansans, restoring trust in government, practical solutions, fiscal responsibility, government transparency, and a government that works for people rather than political insiders. ([campaign homepage](https://stacyrogersforks.com/))
  - Issue-mapping note: direct candidate-controlled issue statement.

- The Meet Stacy page asks why schools struggle, why property taxes rise, and why policies benefit special interests instead of local families. It lists "Put service above politics and people above special interests" among priorities. ([Meet Stacy](https://stacyrogersforks.com/meet-stacy))
  - Issue-mapping note: direct candidate-controlled issue statement; belongs to accountability/governance and property-tax/economic policy.

- On the Hays Post Ellis stop, Rogers said she was running to give people a voice and supported term limits. The same article reports that she would search each state department for inefficiencies and duplications. ([Hays Post](https://hayspost.com/posts/54a43a41-5f58-4499-863d-7844b028f811))
  - Issue-mapping note: secondary reporting of a public campaign stop, relevant to accountability and term limits.

### Issue: Economy, small business, tax incentives, and local control

- The Meet Stacy page lists sunsetting STAR bonds, returning ALBTR cigarette and alcohol tax revenue to counties, protecting against predatory energy projects, and encouraging homegrown business development. ([Meet Stacy](https://stacyrogersforks.com/meet-stacy))
  - Issue-mapping note: direct candidate-controlled issue statement; STAR bonds and ALBTR should not be generalized into a full tax platform without more evidence.

- In Hays Post coverage of the Ellis stop, Rogers said she did not think the whole state should pay for large incentive packages that aid one county or community, citing Chiefs/Royals incentive packages. ([Hays Post](https://hayspost.com/posts/54a43a41-5f58-4499-863d-7844b028f811))
  - Issue-mapping note: secondary public-event evidence of her economic-development posture.

- KCUR summarized Rogers as a Wichita business owner and reported her statement that a first-day executive order would review each department for inefficiencies, mishandling, and fraud. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/politics-elections-and-government/2025-07-24/kansas-governor-race-2026-election-candidates))
  - Issue-mapping note: secondary source, useful for public-platform summary but not a substitute for candidate-controlled text.

### Issue: Education, school choice, special needs, and parental experience

- The Meet Stacy page names school struggles as one of the hard questions Kansans are asking, but does not publish a detailed school-funding plan on the accessible page. ([Meet Stacy](https://stacyrogersforks.com/meet-stacy))
  - Issue-mapping note: candidate-controlled but broad.

- Hays Post reported that Rogers discussed her children attending public and private schools, homeschooling, learning challenges, an IEP dispute, school-board decisions, administrative salaries, possible support for school choice, and money following the student. ([Hays Post](https://hayspost.com/posts/54a43a41-5f58-4499-863d-7844b028f811))
  - Issue-mapping note: secondary public-event evidence; the "might support a form of school choice" phrasing should be kept cautious.

- The Billy Dees podcast listing and transcript identify education failures, support for students with learning challenges, and Rogers's former homeschool-parent experience as topics.
