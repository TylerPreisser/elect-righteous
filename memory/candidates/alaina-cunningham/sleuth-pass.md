**Obs ALC-2 - Came into office by appointment, then won a four-year seat**
- Date: January 5/10, 2023 appointment coverage; November 7/8, 2023 election coverage.

### Issue: Water security / R9 Ranch

**Obs ALC-3 - Stated water is one of Hays' top issues**
- Date: October 25, 2023.
- Body: In her Hays Post candidate Q&A, Cunningham named a sustainable water source as a top issue and described R9 as the framework for building a long-term pipeline supply.
- Source: Hays Post candidate Q&A.
- Issue-mapping note: Stated-position evidence for a water/R9 issue card.
- Tier: SECONDARY, candidate-attributed.

**Obs ALC-4 - R9 quote framed water in survival terms**
- Date: August 29, 2025.
- Body: In Hays-Russell R9 update coverage, Cunningham said, "This is about sustainability and survival," and "without it, we don't survive." The article reported project cost context and litigation timing for the long-term water supply project.
- Source: Hays Post R9 coverage.
- Issue-mapping note: Strong direct quote for water issue. It is a statement, not a vote.
- Tier: SECONDARY.

**Obs ALC-5 - Asked implementation question on R9 pipeline property owners**
- Date: November 15, 2023.
- Body: During a R9 wellfield/pipeline update, Cunningham asked how many property owners would need to be worked with along the 67-mile pipeline. The response estimated roughly 150 to 200 property owners.
- Source: Hays Post R9 wellfield design story.
- Issue-mapping note: Shows operational attention to implementation details; use as action evidence only if the final profile supports secondary-source action items.
- Tier: SECONDARY.

### Issue: Housing, workforce, and development incentives

**Obs ALC-6 - Housing and youth/retiree retention were stated priorities**
- Date: October 2023.
- Body: Cunningham's Hays Post candidate Q&A and Tiger Media profile tied housing supply, youth retention, retirees, and economic growth together. She supported using incentives to make diversified housing feasible and referenced an estimated need for more than 800 housing units by 2030.
- Sources: Hays Post candidate Q&A; Tiger Media candidate profile; Hays Post candidate forum.
- Issue-mapping note: Good stated-position material for a housing/growth issue card.
- Tier: SECONDARY, candidate-attributed.

**Obs ALC-7 - Supported development agreements and longer cost-recoupment period**
- Date: December 2023 coverage.
- Body: Hays Post quoted Cunningham saying, "We've all agreed that growth is what we want," and saying the first developer paying infrastructure costs should have a chance to recoup some costs. The item concerned development-related agreements and infrastructure cost-sharing.
- Source: Hays Post development-agreements story.
- Issue-mapping note: Stated-position/action bridge for development incentives.
- Tier: SECONDARY.

**Obs ALC-8 - Official votes on 2026 housing/incentive items**
- Dates: April 9 and April 23, 2026 minutes embedded in official City of Hays packets.
- Body: The April 9 minutes show Cunningham present by Zoom and voting aye on Exit 157 24-7 TIF/CID actions. The April 23 minutes, included in the May 14 agenda packet, show Cunningham present and voting aye on the Pioneer Run affordable-housing IRB resolution; the packet describes the project as an affordable multifamily complex and says the IRB resolution can add up to 10 points to the developer's Low Income Housing Tax Credit application.
- Sources: City of Hays April 23, 2026 agenda packet; City of Hays May 14, 2026 agenda packet.
- Issue-mapping note: Strong primary action evidence for incentives and housing.
- Tier: PRIMARY.
