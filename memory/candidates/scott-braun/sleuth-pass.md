## Issue-Mapping Notes

### Official status / election cycle

Use official Ellis County 2024 general result: Scott J. Braun, Republican, 11,797 votes for county sheriff; write-ins, 117. Hays Post election-night coverage used the earlier unofficial 11,524 figure. If final UI needs a current election note, use "re-elected 2024; next regular sheriff cycle 2028," not "2026 candidate."

### Jail capacity / public safety operations

This is Braun's clearest recurring local issue. The official county sheriff page states Ellis County maintains a 20,000-square-foot, 72-bed jail. Hays Post coverage attributes to Braun the arguments that the jail cannot always use all nominal beds, out-of-county housing imposes costs and transport/liability burdens, and a new jail is framed by him as a need rather than a want. The Jan. 20, 2026 official commission minutes add a primary action anchor: Braun and Communications Director Jeff Ridgway asked commissioners to authorize Tyler public-safety cloud migration and a jail-management-system upgrade; the motion passed 2-0.

### ICE detainers / immigration enforcement

Official Kansas Legislature records list Braun as an oral proponent for HB 2771 on Feb. 25, 2026. The bill concerned county sheriff enforcement of ICE detainers, insurance coverage, state payment of certain judgments, attorney-general representation, and sheriff authority to enter ICE-related agreements. Hays Post reports Braun said Ellis County signed a memorandum with ICE partly because of federal-funding risk and that the local detention hold is limited to the 48-hour window after local custody. Use the distinction he made: support for honoring detainers in jail context, while saying deputies are not doing street-level immigration investigations.

### Drug forfeiture / opioid enforcement

As Kansas Sheriffs' Association president in 2024, Braun publicly opposed changes that would divert seized drug funds away from local law enforcement and argued forfeiture money funds equipment and controlled buys. Hays Post also reported an April 2025 request by Braun to use about $800 in opioid settlement funds for local-law-enforcement training on fentanyl and opioid-overdose death investigations.

### Mental health / crisis response

Braun repeatedly links jail population and law-enforcement workload to mental-health placement. Hays Post quotes him saying sheriffs statewide struggle with mental-health issues and reports his support for increased High Plains Mental Health Center crisis-intervention funding as an alternative or supplement to incarceration. His LinkedIn profile also lists founding board work with Ellis County Crisis Intervention Team, but treat that as professional/civic background rather than a policy vote.

### Public communication / scams / transparency tools

Hays Post reports Braun promoted a sheriff's office app for direct alerts, tips, inmate information, offender maps, weather/road updates, and vacation patrol requests. A March 2025 ECSO news release quoted by Hays Post warned that the sheriff's office will not demand money or personal information by unsolicited call, text, or email.

### Faith / civic identity

Braun's 2019 candidacy announcement and LinkedIn profile connect him to St. Nicholas of Myra Church Council and the Knights of Columbus. This is relevant biographical/civic context only. Do not infer policy stances from Catholic affiliation or Knights membership.
