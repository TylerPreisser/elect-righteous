## 3. Legislative Action Evidence

### 3.1 HCR 5004 - citizenship voting amendment

**Finding:** HCR 5004 proposes amending Article 5, Section 1 of the Kansas Constitution to require voters to be U.S. citizens, at least 18 years old, and residents of the voting area. Kansas Legislature lists Proctor among original/current sponsors.

- **URL:** https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/hcr5004/
- **Tier:** Primary
- **Accessed:** 2026-05-19
- **Issue mapping:** Election administration / voter eligibility.

**Finding:** House vote on HCR 5004 final action was February 5, 2025: adopted as amended by required two-thirds majority, Yea 90, Nay 28, Absent 7. Proctor's 2025 House vote-record PDF shows he voted yea. Senate final action on March 25, 2025 adopted the resolution 37-3. On April 10, 2025 it was enrolled and presented to the Secretary of State.

- **URLs:** https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/resolutions/HCR5004/history/?filter=votes&per_page=20 and https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/bills/download/?apn=b2025_26/year2/ready_for_publication/vote_record/rep_proctor_pat_1_vote_record_2025.pdf
- **Tier:** Primary
- **Accessed:** 2026-05-19
- **Issue mapping:** Election administration / voter eligibility.

### 3.2 HB 2453 - advance voting and registration deadlines

**Finding:** HB 2453 would require applications for advance voting ballots by the 14th day before Election Day, require in-person advance voting to be completed by noon on the Sunday before Election Day, transmit advance ballots 22 days before an election, and end voter registration 23 days before Election Day.

- **URL:** https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/hb2453/
- **Tier:** Primary
- **Accessed:** 2026-05-19
- **Issue mapping:** Election administration / ballot access and deadlines.

**Finding:** House final action on HB 2453 was February 18, 2026: passed as amended, Yea 86, Nay 38, Absent 1. Roll call #0094 lists Rep. Proctor as Yea. The bill was referred to Senate Federal and State Affairs, had a Senate hearing on March 17, 2026, and died in Senate committee on April 10, 2026.

- **URLs:** https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/bills/HB2453/history/ and https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/vote/?apn=b2025_26/year2/house/legislative_days/day027_20260218/vote_records/0094_HB2453.odt

- **Issue mapping:** Election administration / ballot access and deadlines.
