## Issue-Mapping Notes

### Official status / election-cycle clarity

This profile should be framed as "current USD 489 Board President / current school-board member," not "2026 candidate." Ellis County's 2025 city/school candidate filing PDF listed Curt C. Vajnar for "USD 489 - 3 at-large positions." Ellis County's official Nov. 4, 2025 results show Vajnar first among the USD 489 candidates with 2,549 votes. The 2026 Ellis County candidate filing page lists county and party-committee offices, not USD 489 seats.

### Fiscal oversight / bond oversight

Official action evidence supports this issue. On Oct. 4, 2023, Vajnar voted no on the Nabholz GMP contract for Hays High. On Mar. 24, 2025, he voted yes on a failed motion to look into hiring a project manager for the remainder of the bond. On Jan. 19, 2026, he voted yes on returning $101,708.87 in unallocated contingencies and allowances from the Hays Middle School project to remaining bond projects.

### Transparency / board process

The strongest candidate-attributed statements are from Hays Post Q&As and meeting coverage, not from a campaign social feed. He has described the board's role as policy/oversight and has repeatedly tied decisions to time for review, chain of command, and public accountability. The official action record aligns with that pattern where he dissented or tabled/reviewed items rather than treating agenda recommendations as automatic.

### Nepotism / hiring process

Official minutes for Apr. 24, 2023 show the personnel transaction item passed with Vajnar and Allen Park voting no. Hays Post supplies the surrounding context that the disputed item involved hiring Superintendent Ron Wilson's wife and renewed debate over district nepotism policy. Use both together: official minutes for the vote, Hays Post for the reported reason/context.

### Naming rights / district revenue

Official Aug. 18, 2025 minutes show Vajnar voted no on adding naming of the HHS football field to the agenda and then seconded the successful motion to approve the agenda as presented. Tiger Media Network and Hays Post report his explanation: he removed the Golden Belt Bank field-naming proposal for later discussion, saying the district should control naming and allow other businesses an opportunity.

### Teacher retention / staffing / student support

Hays Post's 2021 and 2025 Q&As are the best candidate-attributed evidence. He ties teacher recruitment/retention to compensation, class size, facilities, and student outcomes. Official Jan. 19, 2026 minutes also show he voted yes on a student support specialist position at Felten Elementary.

### Safety / school resource officers

Hays Post's 2025 candidate Q&A reports Vajnar saying he helped push for the district's first SRO and would continue pushing for a second. I did not locate an official motion/vote in this pass that independently confirms the first-SRO claim.

### Curriculum / practical learning

Existing local harvest and Hays Post coverage show comments on STEM electives, laptop/device choices, and real-world math. Treat this as a secondary-supported issue area unless later work anchors the exact meeting minutes for each item.
