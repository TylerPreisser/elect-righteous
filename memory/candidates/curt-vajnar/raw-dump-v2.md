## Official Status / Election Cycle

### Current office

USD 489's public portal lists Curt Vajnar as "Board President" and includes him among members connected to board retreat, regular meeting, special board meeting, and work session records. The page's `Term:` field is blank, so the exact public-page term line could not be captured there.

Official Jan. 19, 2026 minutes show the board first rejected a motion to elect Ken Brooks as president, 3-4, with Vajnar voting no. The board then elected Ruth Ruder vice-president 7-0. A later motion to elect Curt Vajnar as BOE President carried 4-3, with Allen Park, Curt Vajnar, Jayme Goetz, and Derek Yarmer voting yes and Craig Pallister, Ken Brooks, and Ruth Ruder voting no.

### 2025 election record

Ellis County's 2025 candidate filing PDF listed "Curt C. Vajnar" under "USD 489 - 3 at-large positions." The county's general school-election notice said the Nov. 4, 2025 election included "FOR USD 489 BOARD OF EDUCATION MEMBER - REGULAR TERM vote for three (3)" and listed Curt C. Vajnar. ### Off-cycle status for 2026

Kansas law requires school-board election notices in odd-numbered years, and Kansas municipal/local elections are generally odd-year elections. The 2026 Ellis County candidate filing PDF lists county/party offices and does not include USD 489 board seats. The next regular USD 489 board cycle should therefore be treated as 2027, with Vajnar's seat inferred to run through the 2029 cycle / January 2030 because he won a regular four-year seat in 2025. That term end is an inference from the statute and the 2025 election record, not a direct USD 489 term-field readback.

## Action Evidence

### Bond / facility contract oversight

**Oct. 4, 2023 Nabholz GMP contract**  
Official USD 489 meeting output shows the motion to approve the Nabholz GMP contract as presented passed. Yes votes: Craig Pallister, Lori Hertel, Tammy Wellbrock, Ken Brooks, Meagan Zampieri-Lillpopp. No votes: Allen Park and Curt Vajnar.

Hays Post later reported Vajnar's explanation as not believing he had enough opportunity to educate himself on the contract. Use the official vote as the action anchor and Hays Post as the explanation source.

**Mar. 24, 2025 project manager**  
Official minutes show a motion "to look into hiring a project manager for the remainder of the bond and interview potential candidates under the supervision of the Board of Education." It failed 3-4. Yes: Allen Park, Curt Vajnar, Derek Yarmer. No: Ken Brooks, Meagan Zampieri-Lillpopp, Jayme Goetz, Ruth Ruder.

**Jan. 19, 2026 bond contingency return**  
Official minutes show a motion to accept return of unallocated contingencies and allowances from the Nabholz GMP for the Hays Middle School project in the amount of $101,708.87 to remaining bond projects. Vajnar voted yes; the motion carried 7-0.

### Hiring / nepotism / personnel process

**Apr. 24, 2023 personnel transactions**  
Official meeting output shows personnel transactions passed with yes votes from Craig Pallister, Tammy Wellbrock, Ken Brooks, and Meagan Zampieri-Lillpopp; no votes from Allen Park and Curt Vajnar. Hays Post reported the dispute involved hiring Superintendent Ron Wilson's wife and debate over nepotism policy. Use the official minutes for the vote, Hays Post for the surrounding context.

### Naming rights / sponsorships

**Aug. 18, 2025 agenda handling**  
Official minutes show a motion to approve the agenda with the addition of naming the HHS football field failed 3-3. Vajnar voted no. A second motion to approve the agenda as presented carried 5-1; Vajnar seconded that motion and voted yes.

Tiger Media Network reported the naming-rights offer was from Golden Belt Bank and that Vajnar removed/rescheduled it for later discussion. Tiger quoted/paraphrased him saying the district should be in charge of naming and should let supporting businesses have an opportunity. Hays Post later reported the naming-rights item was postponed again in September and attributed additional comments to Vajnar about citizen ownership of facility names and earmarking revenue for field maintenance.

## Candidate-Attributed Issue Statements

### Student-centered decision rule

Hays Post's 2021 candidate profile says Vajnar described his reason for running as securing the best possible education for all USD 489 students, including his own children, and framed his decision test around what is best for USD 489 students.

### Board role and chain of command

In 2021 and 2025 Hays Post coverage, Vajnar described the board role as policy/oversight rather than daily management. Hays Post's 2025 forum coverage reported him describing a chain of command from teachers/principals/building administration to superintendent and then the board as needed.

### Teacher retention and staffing

Hays Post's 2021 profile reports Vajnar naming teacher shortages and retention among the district's greatest problems. Hays Post's 2025 Q&A reports him discussing teacher raises, class size, and the difficulty of balancing taxes for fixed-income residents with the need to keep competitive teacher compensation.

### Safety / SROs

Hays Post's 2025 Q&A reports Vajnar said he helped push for the district's first resource officer for the 2023-24 year and would continue pushing for a second. No official motion record for the first SRO was captured in this pass.

### Practical curriculum / technology / transportation
