## Section 2 - R9 Ranch / Water Supply / Legal Process

### 2.1 Kansas Department of Agriculture Hays/Russell change applications

**URL**: https://www.agriculture.ks.gov/divisions-programs/division-of-water-resources/water-appropriation/change-applications/hays-change  
**Tier**: primary  
**Publisher**: Kansas Department of Agriculture, Division of Water Resources  
**Accessed**: 2026-05-19

Relevant source facts:
- Hays purchased the approximately 7,000-acre R9 Ranch and 30 water rights in southwestern Edwards County in 1995 for future Hays/Russell public water supply.
- On March 27, 2019, the Chief Engineer contingently approved change applications to convert R9 irrigation rights to municipal use.
- WaterPACK filed a petition for judicial review in Edwards County District Court on May 29, 2019.
- Chief District Judge Bruce Gatterman issued a memorandum and order on June 29, 2022, modifying the Master Order in one notice/hearing respect and otherwise denying WaterPACK's challenges.
- In August 2022 WaterPACK noticed appeal, and in September 2022 the Kansas Supreme Court granted transfer of the case.

- Water supply, state administrative process, litigation.
