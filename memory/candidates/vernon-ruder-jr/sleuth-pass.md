## Corrections / Clarifications

- The older raw notes muddied the 2020 and 2024 election sequence. The best-supported timeline is:
  - 2020 Republican primary: Ruder defeated incumbent Lisa Schlegel and Sherry Dryden.
  - 2020 general: Ruder defeated Democrat Julie Mercer and write-ins.
  - 2024 general: Ruder was listed by Hays Post as incumbent Republican with 11,278 votes and no opponent listed.
- Do not write that Ruder defeated Julie Mercer in 2024. That was a 2020 general-election contest.
- Ruder is part of a public family-office network with Ruth Ruder (USD 489 Board) and Mason Ruder (Hays mayor/city commissioner). This is relevant civic context, not evidence of wrongdoing by itself.
- Social-media claims from opponent/campaign conflict coverage should be treated as claims reported by Hays Post unless independently sourced.

## Working Issue Observations

### Issue: Treasurer Office Administration and Statutory Duties

**Obs V-1 - Official office scope is tax collection/distribution plus motor-vehicle agency work**
- Date: current as of 2026-05-19.
- Body: Ellis County's Treasury page says the treasurer handles tax billing, collection, and distribution for the state, county, cities, and other taxing entities that levy ad valorem or special assessment taxes. It also says the office acts as agent for Kansas Department of Revenue motor-vehicle work for Ellis County residents.
- Source: Ellis County Treasury official page.
- Preliminary tier: PRIMARY.

**Obs V-2 - KCTA lists Ruder as taking office in 2021 and offering tax/payment services**
- Date: current as of 2026-05-19.
- Body: Kansas County Treasurers Association lists Vernon Ruder Jr., took office 2021, and lists services including online tax payments, escrow/partial payments, ACH/wire property-tax payment, notary services, and a 24-hour drop box.
- Source: Kansas County Treasurers Association Ellis County page.
- Preliminary tier: PRIMARY.

### Issue: Motor-Vehicle Facility Fee / User-Fee Funding

**Obs V-3 - Implemented $2.50 walk-in title/registration renewal fee**
- Date: reported 2023-11-20.
- Body: Hays Post reported Ruder implemented a $2.50 fee for walk-in customers for each title registration renewal transaction. Ruder framed it as a state-authorized facility fee to fund motor-vehicle operations locally.
- Source: Hays Post motor-vehicle fee article.
- Preliminary tier: SECONDARY, with candidate-attributed quotes.

**Obs V-4 - Framed fee as reducing reliance on property taxpayers**
- Date: reported 2023-11-20.
- Body: Ruder said the motor-vehicle department should be funded with motor-vehicle money and said the fee helps people who pay property taxes by shifting some costs to motor-vehicle users.
- Source: Hays Post motor-vehicle fee article.
- Preliminary tier: SECONDARY.

**Obs V-5 - Claimed fee generated more than $50,000 and helped reduce budget from $452,000 to $367,000**
- Date: reported 2023-11-20.
- Body: Hays Post reported Ruder estimated the added fee would probably generate more than $50,000 that year and said he told commissioners he reduced the treasury budget by $85,000 from 2022 to 2023.

## Donor / Funding Notes

- KPDC says county offices are covered by the Campaign Finance Act, and local candidates file Appointment of Treasurer forms with the county clerk/county election commissioner.
- County-office forms page says candidates may file an affidavit of exemption if they expect to receive/spend less than $1,000 in the primary and less than $1,000 in the general.
- No online Ruder donor list, campaign-finance report, campaign website donation page, or active campaign committee document was located in this pass.
