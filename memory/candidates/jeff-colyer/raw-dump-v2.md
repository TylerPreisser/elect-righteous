## Status Summary

Jeff Colyer is running an active Republican campaign for Kansas governor. Kansas Public Disclosure Commission/Kansas.gov statewide 2026 records list "Colyer, Jeff" under gubernatorial candidates. His Appointment of Treasurer form lists office sought as Governor and was executed May 12, 2025.

Official ballot wording needs caution. The Kansas Secretary of State candidate-information page says candidates for national, state, legislative, and judicial offices must file with the Secretary of State and are not considered filed until their petition or declaration and fee are received. It lists the 2026 primary filing deadline as 12:00 p.m. Monday, June 1, 2026. The official SOS candidate-list endpoint returned a JavaScript/robot-verification page in text capture, so this pass did not obtain a machine-readable official SOS filed-candidate list confirming final ballot placement. Treat Colyer as an active gubernatorial campaign/finance filer, with final filed-candidate/ballot status pending official readback.

Colyer's prior public office is well documented. Kansas Historical Society identifies him as Kansas's 47th governor, sworn in Jan. 31, 2018 after Gov. Sam Brownback resigned. It also records service in the Kansas House, Kansas Senate, and as lieutenant governor. Official 2018 Kansas primary results show Colyer/Tracey Mann lost the Republican governor primary to Kobach/Hartman by 336 votes.

### Prior office and 2018 election history

**Sources:** s-kansapedia, s-nga-profile, s-sos-2018-primary-results, s-kcur-launch.

Kansas Historical Society identifies Colyer as Kansas's 47th governor, sworn in Jan. 31, 2018 after Gov. Sam Brownback resigned. It also identifies prior service in the Kansas House, Kansas Senate, and as lieutenant governor.

**Issue mapping:** biography, experience, election history. Correct the local-memory shorthand from "roughly 345" to official 336 if used in downstream copy.

## Issue Area 1 - Taxes, Affordability, Fraud, and Property Taxes

**Sources:** s-campaign-commitment, s-kcur-launch, s-social-harvest-local.

The current campaign's first pillar is "Economic Strength / Lower Costs. Higher Pay." The 25-point plan says Colyer would:

- End the Democrats' decade of welfare fraud.
- Make Kansas a high-wage, low-tax state again.
- Place "a real lid" on property taxes.
- Deliver fiscal responsibility and balanced budgets.
- Bring high-paying manufacturing and tech jobs to Kansas.

KCUR/KMUW launch coverage reports that Colyer said he held the line on taxes, including property taxes, while governor, and listed reducing government spending, cutting taxes, bolstering small businesses, protecting farmers, and bringing tech/manufacturing jobs among launch priorities.

The local social harvest found accessible X/campaign items on SNAP fraud, unemployment-insurance fraud, Sedgwick County property valuations/mill levies/reappraisals, higher wages/lower taxes, and lesser prairie chicken regulation/property rights.

**Issue mapping:** taxes, affordability, property taxes, welfare/SNAP/UI fraud, economic development, small business, agriculture/property regulation.

**Evidence caution:** The current plan is campaign-level. No draft bill text or fiscal model was found in this pass.

## Issue Area 3 - Faith, Family, Abortion, Guns, and Religious Liberty

**Sources:** s-campaign-commitment, s-campaign-current, s-campaign-old-announcements, s-ksleg-sb284, s-ksleg-sb284-enrolled, s-social-harvest-local.

The current campaign plan groups several topics under "Faith, Values & Life / Faith & Family First." It includes "Protect God-given rights," "Protect the unborn and provide options for young mothers," and "Protect lawful gun owners and hunters from being targeted by the left."

The older campaign announcements archive remains live and includes 2021 material on abortion, KU adult stem-cell research, oil/gas, COVID-era closures, and conservative identity. Treat this as older candidate-controlled archive material, not necessarily new 2026 platform detail.

Primary action evidence: Kansas Legislature's SB 284 page identifies the bill as creating the Adoption Protection Act and updating the Kansas adoption and relinquishment act. Bill history states it was approved by the governor on May 18, 2018, with the enrolled bill effective July 1, 2018. The enrolled text says, within limits of federal law, no child-placement agency shall be required to participate in placement where the proposed placement would violate the agency's sincerely held religious beliefs.

**Issue mapping:** abortion/life issues, religious liberty, foster/adoption policy, firearms, sex/gender policy where campaign plan names it.

**Neutral framing:** Do not infer Colyer's current church membership or personal religious state from campaign language, Catholic-school background, or SB 284 signature. State the public act and candidate-controlled framing only.
