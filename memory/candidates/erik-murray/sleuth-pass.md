## Issue-Mapping Notes

- **Healthcare:** campaign issue page supports universal coverage, restoring ACA cuts, protecting/expanding Medicaid, and framing health access as fiscally responsible. YouTube/social posts also say healthcare is a right.
- **Education:** campaign issue page supports full funding for public education, special education, trades training, and college pathways. YouTube posts discuss Sumner Academy and public education as a multigenerational family anchor.
- **Housing / community development:** campaign issue page supports more housing, construction incentives, infrastructure for neighborhoods, and red-tape reduction. KSHB reporting on Indian Springs/Midtown Station gives the strongest action record tied to his development work.
- **Immigration:** newer campaign issue page fills an older gap. It says immigration is an economic engine and calls for secure, orderly borders, humane and efficient administration, clear pathways, real enforcement, and a system built for the 21st-century economy.
- **Taxes / wages / affordability:** campaign issue page supports cutting taxes on working families, exploring an exemption for the first $75,000 to $100,000 of income, requiring corporations and the ultra-wealthy to pay a fair share, and raising the federal minimum wage to at least $15 per hour.
- **Monopoly / antitrust / small business:** campaign issue page calls for enforcing antitrust law and protecting workers, small businesses, and consumers from large firms that fix prices or crush competition.
- **Tariffs / agriculture:** campaign issue page says tariffs are a tax and calls for eliminating broad tariffs, using targeted tariffs only for specific national-interest cases, and opening global markets for Kansas farmers, ranchers, manufacturers, and consumers. YouTube and campaign-link RSS items tie farm bankruptcies, farm closures, tariffs, rural hospitals, and affordability together.
- **Democracy / civil rights / reproductive freedom:** campaign issue page calls for transparency, voting-rights protection, privacy rights, restoring reproductive freedom, and equal protection regardless of gender, race, age, orientation, or zip code.
- **Government reform / ethics:** campaign issue page supports ending Citizens United, banning congressional stock trading, a code of ethics across all three branches, lobbying/revolving-door reform, and accountability.
- **Veterans / public service:** campaign issue page supports a fully funded VA and support for veterans, teachers, first responders, and healthcare workers.
- **Religious liberty:** no specific religious-liberty policy plank found. Biography says Murray attended Fellowship Christian Church with his grandmother and St. Patrick's Catholic School growing up; do not infer current church membership or policy position from that.
- **Guns / Second Amendment:** no candidate-controlled gun-policy plank found in the reviewed materials.
- **Energy / AI:** social/Youtube posts discuss wind energy alternatives and AI; the main issue page does not yet provide a detailed energy or technology policy.
