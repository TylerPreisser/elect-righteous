## Issue-Mapping Notes

- **Public education / school funding:** Strongest issue cluster. Candidate-controlled pages call for top-25 teacher pay, improved teacher benefits, fully funded junior college and technical-school tuition, two healthy meals daily for public-school students, no cell phones during school hours, reduced administrative bloat, support-staff investment, and opposition to vouchers/privatization/private-school tax breaks.
- **School athletics / LGBTQ policy:** The older still-live `/to-do-list` page explicitly says "Support transgender rights but oppose transgender girls competing in sports with biological girls." The newer `/platform` page uses broader wording: "Ensure fair and consistent standards in school athletics." WIBW and EIN Presswire both reported/restated the more explicit sports position. Downstream prose should cite the exact page/version used and avoid inferring broader LGBTQ policy beyond the stated athletics item.
- **Health and fitness:** Candidate-controlled pages and launch release emphasize daily physical education, eliminating padded football under age 13, farm-to-table school nutrition, obesity prevention, personal freedom in healthcare decisions, and a culture of health/wellness. This maps cleanly to Tuley's public identity as an author/personal trainer but does not establish a health-policy governing record.
- **Economic and tax policy:** Candidate-controlled pages call for legalizing marijuana with tax revenue for education and health programs, reforming corporate tax incentives for out-of-state corporations, prioritizing Kansas-based businesses, and opposing tax cuts used as political tools.
- **Infrastructure and energy:** Candidate-controlled pages support infrastructure projects using Kansas labor, wind and solar energy development, reduced plastic use, and paperless state government operations.
- **Voting / governance:** Candidate-controlled pages call for limited use of executive orders and pardons as political tools, accessible voting with secure systems, and long-term investment over austerity/short-term cuts. The older `/to-do-list` page specifically mentions electronic voting options; the newer `/platform` page says accessible voting while maintaining secure and reliable systems.
- **Immigration / border:** Candidate-controlled pages use broad language supporting secure but accessible/practical/humane border policy for hardworking individuals and families. No detailed implementation plan was found.
- **Rural hospitals / Medicaid expansion:** Kansas Reflector debate coverage reported Tuley discussing a southwest Kansas hospital official and rural hospital profitability since 2017. No candidate-controlled detailed Medicaid-expansion or rural-hospital platform plank was confirmed in this pass.
- **Abortion, gun policy, religious liberty, child care, foster care, policing/prisons, water/Ogallala, property taxes, tribal issues, Kansas Supreme Court selection:** No detailed candidate-controlled statements were confirmed in the reviewed campaign pages, local harvest, or current verification.
