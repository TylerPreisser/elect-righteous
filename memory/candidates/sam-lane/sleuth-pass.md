## Scope note
This pass covers public, candidate-controlled, official, and indexed material for Sam Lane, Democratic candidate for Kansas Secretary of State. ## Official status snapshot
- Kansas Secretary of State candidate-information pages say state and statewide candidates file with the Secretary of State, and that a candidate is not considered filed until the petition or declaration and fee are received. The primary filing deadline is noon on June 1, 2026. Source: https://www.sos.ks.gov/elections/candidate-information.html and https://sos.ks.gov/media/press-releases/2026/05-01-26-one-month-countdown-until-candidate-filing-deadline.html

Treat this as an access blocker, not evidence against filing.
- Johnson County Election Office lists Samuel Lane as a non-incumbent, non-elected-official Democratic candidate from Shawnee for the August 4, 2026 primary, Office Level: State Offices; Office Jurisdiction/Position: Secretary of State; term 4 years, term end 2031. Source: https://www.jocoelection.org/candidates-elected-officials/samuel-lane
- Kansas Public Disclosure Commission / Kansas.gov statewide-office index lists "Lane, Samuel" under Secretary of State candidates and links his Appointment of Treasurer and 202601 receipts-and-expenditures report. The index says it was last updated January 22, 2026. Source: https://www.kansas.gov/ethics/CFAScanned/StWide/2026ElecCycle/SWLinks2026EC.htm

## Issue mapping notes

### Election administration / voter access
Why it maps: This is the central campaign-controlled issue area and directly relates to the Secretary of State office.

Observed evidence:
- Campaign home says Lane is running because the Kansas Secretary of State oversees elections, and that fair elections are necessary for a democratic republic. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/
- Campaign home says he has met with several county election commissioners/county clerks and, if elected, would visit every county election office in Kansas. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/
- Secretary-of-state explainer says the office handles administrative duties including business registration and legal publications, but emphasizes election oversight. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/what-does-the-secretary-of-state-do
- Campaign issues page addresses voter fraud, voter suppression, HB 2438, mail-ballot deadlines, the SAVE Act / SAVE America Act, ballot-location security, and ballot-location closures. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/issues
- Kansas Legislature HB 2438 page describes the bill as imposing restrictions on websites that can accept and transmit voter-registration applications. Source: https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/hb2438/
- Kansas Legislature SB 4 page says it requires return of advance voting ballots by 7:00 p.m. on election day and was veto-overridden. Source: https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/sb4/

Social/online relevance:
- Bluesky profile bio connects due process, checks and balances, the people's voice, and fair and accurate elections. Source: https://public.api.bsky.app/xrpc/app.bsky.actor.getProfile?actor=voiceinavacuum.bsky.social
- On March 25, 2026, Lane posted opposition to the SAVE Act / SAVE America Act and linked an explainer. Source: https://bsky.app/profile/voiceinavacuum.bsky.social/post/3mhw77rifik2s
- On April 12, 2026, Lane posted that he drove to Oberlin while working a full-time nonpolitical job and, if elected, would drive across Kansas to keep polling locations open. Source: https://bsky.app/profile/voiceinavacuum.bsky.social/post/3mjbey3c4as2m
- On November 4, 2025, he posted that some counties close polls at 5 p.m. and called that "something I want to change." Source: https://bsky.app/profile/voiceinavacuum.bsky.social/post/3m4remghm3c2s

### Election security / paper trail
Why it maps: Lane distinguishes voter-fraud claims from election-system maintenance and says paper ballots/audit trails are part of his approach.

Observed evidence:
- Campaign issues page says the current system appears to work, but should be reviewed and maintained; it says he would seek "a thorough and unbroken paper trail" at every election site and refers to full logic and accuracy testing before ballots are processed. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/issues
- On October 19, 2025, he posted that Kansas is a paper-ballot state and told voters to check machine-printed ballots. Source: local social harvest and Bluesky post https://bsky.app/profile/voiceinavacuum.bsky.social/post/3m3lgre7oik23
- On August 6, 2025, he posted that he had walked through several county election offices and seen archived paper ballots; local harvest summarizes his point that Kansas has systems that make elections hard to hack. Source: social-harvest.md

### Ballot-site security / county resources
Why it maps: This is about practical election administration and county-level resources, not a general public-safety plank.

Observed evidence:
- Campaign issues page says he wants unobtrusive security around ballot drop boxes, tactful plainclothes security at each ballot location, and a plan to protect people and paper ballots in case of a bomb threat. It also says Kansas counties pay for their own elections with limited state support, so state resources would matter. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/issues
- Campaign home says he would visit county election offices to hear what resources they need from Topeka. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/

### Civil liberties / privacy / identity documents
Why it maps: Lane connects due process, privacy, government identification rules, and voter-access barriers to the Secretary of State campaign.

Observed evidence:
- Bluesky profile bio names due process and checks and balances. Source: Bluesky API profile.
- On January 30, 2026, local harvest captured Lane saying digital privacy is "a value I intend to take into the Secretary of State's office." Source: https://bsky.app/profile/voiceinavacuum.bsky.social/post/3mdm7fwsvqc2c
- Campaign issues page discusses Kansas SB 244 in the context of driver's-license sex markers and possible voter-registration documentation problems. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/issues
- Kansas Legislature SB 244 page says the bill defines gender as biological sex at birth for statutory construction, directs driver-license and birth-certificate correction/reissuance when necessary, and had a veto override. Source: https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/sb244/

### Abortion policy
Why it maps: Lane has an explicit candidate-controlled "My Other Views" abortion tab. The page says it is not as immediately relevant to Secretary of State, so it should be presented as a personal/civic view rather than an office-power promise.

Observed evidence:
- Campaign "My Other Views" page says Lane does not like to dwell on personal feelings about abortion, calls abortion policy the less complicated part for him, and criticizes abortion bans for their effect on medical practice. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/my-other-views
- No elected-office vote or official action by Lane on abortion was found.

### Guns / Second Amendment
Why it maps: Lane has an explicit candidate-controlled "My Other Views" guns tab. It is relevant to voter knowledge but not directly tied to Secretary of State duties.

Observed evidence:
- Campaign "My Other Views" page says that when he says he loves civil liberties, he does not make an exception for gun rights. It also says that if he were running for a lawmaker office, he would be "alright with some basic gun control legislation" such as closing loopholes in the background-check system, but notes he is not running for a lawmaker office. Source: https://www.samuellane.org/my-other-views
- No elected-office vote or official action by Lane on firearms was found.
