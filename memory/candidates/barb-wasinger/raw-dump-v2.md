## Issue Area 1 - Water / Local Infrastructure

### Action evidence: HB 2433 sponsor and signed law

**Sources:** s-hb2433, s-hb2433-house-vote, s-hays-hb2433-signed.

The official HB 2433 page describes the bill as clarifying county authority over water transfer or appropriation by placing such authority, except domestic use, with the chief engineer and water transfer hearing panel. The bill page identifies Rep. Barb Wasinger as both original sponsor and current sponsor and marks the bill "Signed by Governor."

House final action on Feb. 10, 2026 passed 116-6. Wasinger appears in the yea list.

Hays Post reported on Mar. 20, 2026 that Gov. Kelly signed the bill; the article says the bill was sponsored and championed by Wasinger and would prevent Edwards County from including water transfer regulations in local codes or acting as its own water authority. Hays Post tied the bill to the Hays/Russell R9 Ranch water-rights dispute, reporting that Edwards County fought the water transfer in court for 11 years, costing Hays and Russell millions.

**Issue mapping:** Water security, county/state authority, Hays/Russell R9 Ranch.

**Neutral framing:** This is a concrete local-result item. It also involved opposition testimony from Edwards County and water/agriculture voices, so downstream prose should avoid one-sided "win" phrasing unless attributed to the campaign.
