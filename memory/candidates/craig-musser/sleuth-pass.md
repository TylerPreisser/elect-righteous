## Issue-Mapped Public Statements

### Healthcare

- Campaign issues page supports universal preventative care, naming routine blood tests, cancer screening, and vaccinations.
- It calls for lower medical costs through nonprofit-medicine definition changes and standardized billing/reimbursement practices.
- It supports broader use of telehealth and technology for non-urgent care where suitable.
- It says mental healthcare should be treated with the same urgency and compassion as physical illness.
- It proposes government action to improve access to healthy food and physical fitness, including examples such as subsidizing farmers to provide healthy foods at lower cost or tax incentives for community physical activity.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled issue statements. This is one of the most developed policy areas on the current site.

### Personal freedoms / women / child welfare / LGBTQ-related equal treatment

- Campaign issues page says Kansans value privacy and support others' rights to make decisions for themselves and their families without government or fellow-citizen input from people who disagree.
- It says that, if elected, Musser would uphold privacy and bodily autonomy by equally supporting community members regardless of race, religion, sexual orientation, or gender.
- It says government should not dictate to women what is best for their lives and bodies.
- Its "Being Pro Child" section says being pro-child does not mean forcing women to give birth or encouraging them to relinquish children; it emphasizes child welfare, displaced and mistreated children, adult adoptees, and former foster youth.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled statements. For v2 issue cards, treat abortion/women/child welfare and LGBTQ equal-treatment language as stated positions; do not collapse them into slogans or infer views beyond the text.

### Religious liberty / public education

- Biography page says Musser attended Church of the Brethren while growing up. No current church affiliation was found.
- Education section says he learned morals from his parents and church, quotes Jefferson on religion as a matter between a person and God, and says state-funded education should be free to teach about all world religions but promote none.

**Mapping reason:** The education page supplies a direct statement about church/state boundaries in state-funded education. It should not be used as a current worship-affiliation claim.

### Social Security

- Campaign issues page says Social Security must be maintained so everyone who paid in receives the income and benefits their money provided others.
- It calls for a substantial and well-trained workforce, keeping the federal budget in line to honor Treasury notes, and raising the FICA cap to match what high contributors take out.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled federal-program statement. Map to Social Security / seniors / budget.

### Education

- Campaign issues page supports more public funding to attract, educate, and continuously train teachers.
- It says state and federal requirements should come with resources, and some resources should be provided to schools without political strings or agendas.
- It says student loans should be reformed so students and parents are not required to take high-interest loans to fund job-needed education.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled statements on education funding, school discretion, church/state boundaries, and student debt.

### Budget / taxes / IRS

- Campaign issues page supports public investment in infrastructure, transportation networks, water and sewer systems, energy grids, telecommunications, technology, and energy innovation.
- It says the tax code and the way funds are directed need simplification.
- It says the IRS should function primarily as a service, with trained staff available for guidance and enforcement focused on complex returns and large unpaid tax obligations.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled statements. Map to taxes, budget, infrastructure, and government administration.

### Agriculture

- Campaign issues page says market stabilization is important for farmers and suggests ideas such as limiting government changes that upset markets during crucial farming timeframes and expanding into new markets.
- It asks farmers and ranchers for information about equipment, raw materials, markets, processing, monopolies, and government.
- It says Musser does not need or want money from farmers, only knowledge and opinions.

**Mapping reason:** Direct candidate-controlled agriculture statements, but note that they are broad and consultative. No specific Farm Bill, crop insurance, water, or trade-policy detail was confirmed.
