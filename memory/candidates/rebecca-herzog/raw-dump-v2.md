### Statutory Duties and Public Access

K.S.A. 19-1201 establishes county registers of deeds as four-year elected offices. K.S.A. 19-1204 gives the office custody/preservation and recording duties. K.S.A. 19-1202 gives the register personnel supervision and budget-submission duties within county-approved limits.

Ellis County's own Register of Deeds page says the office records qualified public documents and maintains accurate indexes for land ownership. The county page also says most office records are open to the public, with a stated Sales Validation Questionnaire exception.

The Document Recording Process page describes how documents are examined for apparent errors, assigned recording information, and entered into indexes by name and legal description. The page also says appraiser and clerk tax-roll processes depend on Register of Deeds records.

### Records, Preservation, E-Recording, and Fraud Alert

The 2025 budget book says the Register of Deeds office does e-recording through Simplifile and CSC. It says the office records several hundred types of documents, including deeds, mortgages and liens, oil and gas leases, plats, military discharge records, powers of attorney, corporation records, cemetery records, and former county school records.

The same budget section says some earliest records date to the 1800s and describes the office as serving the public by recording and preserving county records. It also notes sensitive records exceptions and says the office has fraud alert notification within its software.

The RNS page announces a free service for property owners to receive notices when documents are recorded against their property.
