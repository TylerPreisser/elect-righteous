# KC Ohaebosim

## Who They Are
KC Ohaebosim was added because multiple public reports and the Holscher campaign identify him as Cindy Holscher's lieutenant governor running mate. Reports identify Ohaebosim as a Wichita Democrat elected to the Kansas House in 2016, a product of Wichita public schools, a Wichita State University and Newman University graduate, and a data analyst. Reports also note he was named regional legislator of the year by the National Black Caucus of State Legislators in 2022 and 2025.
