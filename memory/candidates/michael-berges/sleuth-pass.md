## Issue-Mapped Observations

### Observation 1 - Filed incumbent for 2026 District 1

**What was observed:** Ellis County's current candidate-filings PDF lists Michael Berges for Commission District 1 as a Republican. Ellis County's official commission page lists him as the current First District commissioner.

**Issue mapping:** Ballot accuracy / who-they-are. This closes the earlier project uncertainty about whether he belongs on the 2026 Ellis County ballot.

**Source tier:** Primary.

### Observation 2 - Voted yes on 2026 budget exceeding revenue-neutral rate

**What was observed:** September 9, 2025 official minutes record Berges present by telephone, public hearing comments from residents, and a named roll call on the 2026 General Fund and Fire District budgets exceeding the revenue-neutral rates. Roll call: Berges yes, Younger yes, Leiker yes. Resolution 2025-14 says the county found it necessary to exceed the RNR.

**Issue mapping:** County budget, taxes, services. This is the clearest primary-source fiscal action for the v2 profile.

**Source tier:** Primary; Hays Post provides readable secondary context about the 1.96 mill increase and public criticism.

### Observation 3 - Advanced the 2026 budget to public hearing and framed subdivision road work

**What was observed:** August 19, 2025 official minutes show Berges moved to authorize publication of the 2026 General Fund and Fire District budgets for September 9 hearings. The same meeting shows Berges opening a "Subdevelopment Road Discussion," identifying 21 county-maintained subdivisions, and asking for a later cost estimate for 280th Ave after public input.

**Issue mapping:** Budget process, road maintenance, subdivision infrastructure.

**Source tier:** Primary.

### Observation 4 - Supported county compensation adjustments, including his own office's salary step

**What was observed:** January 13, 2026 minutes record Leiker moving and Berges seconding Resolution 2026-04, setting 2026 elected-official salary steps. The resolution lists County Commissioner District 1 at Pay Grade 35, Step 4, $20,746, effective January 4, 2026. Motion passed 3-0.

**Issue mapping:** Governance, compensation, employee retention, budget transparency. The item should be described neutrally as a countywide elected-official salary resolution, not singled out as personal enrichment absent evidence of unusual process.

**Source tier:** Primary.

### Observation 5 - Voted yes on Fairview Patch final plat while Leiker dissented

**What was observed:** July 1, 2025 minutes record a motion by Younger and second by Berges to approve the Fairview Patch final plat and Resolution 2025-12. The motion passed 2-1; Leiker was the no vote. Berges signed the final resolution as chair.

**Issue mapping:** Subdivision growth, land use, county infrastructure. This is useful contrast inside the same race because Leiker's v2 artifact treats this vote as one of his clearest divergences.

**Source tier:** Primary.

### Observation 6 - Public safety / courts: opioid fund use and recovery court grant

**What was observed:** February 17, 2026 minutes record Berges present for a recovery court grant presentation, discussion of jail capacity, and a motion by Leiker seconded by Berges to approve approximately $1,828.29 in opioid funds for three sheriff's officers to attend the Kansas Narcotics Officers Association yearly conference. Motion passed 3-0.

**Issue mapping:** Public safety, substance-use response, courts, jail pressure. This provides a concrete action item without inferring broader criminal-justice ideology.

**Source tier:** Primary.

### Observation 7 - Candidate-attested 2022 platform centered infrastructure, budget stabilization, sheriff/jail, Grow Hays, and intergovernmental cooperation

**What was observed:** In the 2022 Hays Post candidate profile, Berges supported earmarking sales-tax revenue for infrastructure upkeep, named bridges and roads, described sales-tax extension/permanent-sales-tax questions as future options, identified sheriff/jail facility needs as a major project, supported more proactive economic development funding through Grow Hays, and discussed cooperation with Ellis, Hays, Victoria, USD 489, and other entities.

**Issue mapping:** Use candidate-attested Q&A as "stated" source for budget, roads, public safety, and economic development issue cards.

**Source tier:** Primary for candidate's own answers as published; secondary for Hays Post framing.
