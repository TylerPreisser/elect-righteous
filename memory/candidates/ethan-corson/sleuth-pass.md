## Issue-Mapping Notes

- **Public education / school funding:** Strong candidate-controlled emphasis. Campaign page leads with "Better Schools" and says Corson would work with both parties to make Kansas schools among the best in America. Legislative actions include a yea vote on SB 44 expanding Kansas Promise Scholarship eligibility, yea votes on SB 45 school accreditation graduation-rate calculation, and a nay vote on SB 47 school-board governance changes. Current social embed also surfaced posts about Shawnee Mission North and Shawnee Mission South.
- **Taxes / cost of living / economic development:** Strong campaign emphasis. Campaign page says Corson worked to eliminate the food sales tax, cut property taxes, remove taxes on Social Security, and deliver targeted tax relief. Legislative actions include sponsorship of SB 215 on homestead property-tax refund changes, sponsorship of SB 216 establishing paid sick time, and votes on 2025 tax measures. The campaign biography connects his U.S. Department of Commerce / International Trade Administration work to business recruitment and helping Kansas farmers ship products abroad.
- **Abortion / reproductive policy:** Current campaign-site embedded X feed includes a reproductive-rights post excerpt. Official vote evidence: Corson voted nay on HB 2062 in 2025, concerning child support for unborn children and unborn/stillborn income-tax exemptions, and nay on HB 2729 in 2026, concerning Woman's-Right-to-Know Act forms and notices. Both measures passed/overrode vetoes. A full issue page or detailed gubernatorial platform page on abortion was not found.
- **Marriage, family, and LGBTQ policy:** No marriage-specific campaign plank was found. Official vote evidence shows Corson voted nay on SB 63 in 2025, nay on SB 76 in 2025, nay on SB 244 in 2026, and nay on HB 2311 in 2025. These votes map to gender-transition care for minors, school name/pronoun requirements, biological-sex/public-building/private-space rules, and foster-care/religious-or-moral-belief placement policy.
- **Healthcare / rural access / mental health:** Campaign and debate material emphasize care without going broke or driving hours. Official action evidence includes yea votes on rural emergency hospital skilled-nursing waivers and nurse anesthetist scope, plus campaign snippets about school mental health and mental health for parents, first responders, and farmers.
- **Public safety / first responders:** Campaign site says Corson supports first responders. Official action evidence includes SB 477, sponsored by Corson, establishing a Kansas first responder scholarship program; HB 2479, where Corson voted yea on domestic-violence/stalking/protective-order electronic monitoring with victim notification; and SB 206, requested by Corson, concerning distribution of controlled substances causing great bodily harm or death.
- **Elections / democracy / ethics:** Official action evidence includes nay votes on SB 4 advance-ballot return deadline changes, SB 5 federal election-fund restrictions, SB 6 ranked-choice voting prohibition, and SCR 1611 direct election of Supreme Court justices. Corson sponsored SB 313, prohibiting legislators from trading stocks and other securities during legislative sessions.
