## Status Summary

Dinah Sykes is an active Kansas state senator and Senate Minority Leader running as a Democrat for Kansas Commissioner of Insurance in 2026. KPDC lists "Sykes, Dinah" under statewide Insurance Commissioner candidates for the 2026 election cycle and links her appointment-of-treasurer form. The form identifies her office sought as Insurance Commissioner and party as Democratic.

Direct Kansas SOS candidate-list readback was blocked in this environment. Kansas SOS's candidate-information page confirms that statewide candidates file with the Secretary of State and are not considered filed until the declaration or petition and fee are received. A Douglas County election-office candidate export printed May 19, 2026, says statewide-office candidates on that list have filed documents with the State of Kansas and lists Dinah Sykes for Commissioner of Insurance, Democratic, Lenexa, filed Jan. 8, 2026. Because the June 1, 2026 filing deadline has not yet passed, downstream language should say "filed/running" rather than final ballot nominee.

Kansas Legislature confirms Sykes is the current senator for District 21 and Senate Minority Leader. Kansas SOS official 2024 general results confirm her reelection to Senate District 21 with 29,925 votes, 57.14%.

## Issue Evidence

### Insurance affordability and consumer protection

**Candidate-controlled statement:** The campaign site says Kansas deserves an insurance commissioner who puts people first and frames her campaign around fairness and affordability.

**Action / public campaign evidence:**

- Sykes filed for Insurance Commissioner and appointed a treasurer on Jan. 8, 2026.
- Kansas Reflector launch coverage quotes her saying, "The cost of health insurance in Kansas is simply out of control."
- Launch coverage says she pledged not to take a single dime from the insurance industry.
- Campaign site lists endorsements from former Insurance Commissioners Sandy Praeger and Kathleen Sebelius.

**Source IDs:** s-kpdc-sykes-treasurer, s-campaign-home, s-kansas-reflector-launch.

### Health care access and insurance coverage

**Candidate-controlled / official statement:** The current campaign centers health-insurance costs. The Kansas Senate Democrats profile says she advocates expanding health-care access.

**Action evidence:**

- Kansas Legislature bill page for SB 219 lists Sykes among sponsors of a bill concerning insurance coverage for supplemental and diagnostic breast examinations.
- Kansas Reflector launch coverage links her insurance campaign to health insurance costs and coverage problems.

**Source IDs:** s-campaign-home, s-kssenatedems-profile, s-ksleg-sb219, s-kansas-reflector-launch.

### Working-family affordability, wages, and taxes

**Candidate-controlled / official statement:** The campaign site frames affordability as a core value. Governor's-office release quotes Sykes in a joint statement backing a $15 minimum wage and saying Kansas workers should not make less than Missouri workers.

**Action evidence:**

- SB 216 lists Sykes among sponsors of a paid sick time bill.
- SB 218 lists Sykes among sponsors of a Kansas minimum wage bill.
- SB 217 lists Sykes among sponsors of a bill excluding Social Security payments from Kansas adjusted gross income.
- 2025 vote record shows Sykes voting against HB 2036, an income-tax-rate bill listed in her vote record.

**Source IDs:** s-governor-minwage, s-ksleg-sb216, s-ksleg-sb218, s-ksleg-sb217, s-ksleg-vote-record-2025.

### Public schools and education

**Stated / public record:** Kansas Senate Democrats profile says Sykes has focused on public-school funding and early childhood education. Her 2023 Capitol Update criticized voucher policy and Medicaid refusal alongside other legislative issues.

**Action evidence:**

- 2025 vote record shows Sykes voted against SB 75, which her roll-call record describes as a tax-credit bill.
- 2025 vote record shows Sykes voted against HB 2045, which her roll-call record describes as an education savings account bill.
- Kansas Legislature profile lists her as Ranking Minority Member on the Senate Committee on Education.

**Source IDs:** s-kssenatedems-profile, s-joc-post-2023, s-ksleg-profile, s-ksleg-vote-record-2025.

### Abortion / reproductive policy

**Stated / public record:** Secondary coverage shows Sykes speaking at a reproductive-rights rally in 2024. Her 2023 candidate-attributed Capitol Update criticized abortion restrictions.

**Action evidence:**

- 2025 vote record shows Sykes voted against SB 63, listed in the record as concerning an abortion-coercion crime, abortion reporting, and ultrasound-related provisions.
- Kansas Reflector coverage documented her 2024 reproductive-rights rally remarks during veto-override activity.

**Source IDs:** s-ksleg-vote-record-2025, s-reflector-abortion-2024, s-joc-post-2023.

### LGBTQ policy

**Action evidence:**

**Source IDs:** s-ksleg-vote-record-2025, s-joc-post-2023, s-advocate-2024.

### Governance, civility, and political safety

**Stated / public record:** KCUR interviewed Sykes after the assassination of Minnesota Rep. Melissa Hortman. Existing local harvest records her discussing safety concerns, divisiveness, and going after policy rather than people.

**Action evidence:**

**Source IDs:** s-kcur-safety-2025, s-ksleg-vote-record-2025, s-threadreader-redistricting-2022.
