## Primary-Source Leads By Issue

### Insurance Affordability / Insurance Regulation / Prescription Drug Costs

- Campaign site says Hawkins wants a fair and transparent insurance system, lower costs by increasing options, less red tape, a taxpayer-friendly Kansas Department of Insurance, and stronger action against insurance and securities fraud.
- Kansas Legislature profile lists Hawkins' occupation as insurance agent.
- KPDC report shows 2025 finance totals and contributions from insurance, health care, pharmaceutical/PBM, and related PAC/business entities including NAIFA Kansas PAC, Shelter Insurance Kansas PAC, Pharmaceutical Care Management Association, UnitedHealth Group PAC, CVS Health, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Kansas City PAC, BlueCross BlueShield Kansas, Lockton Management, New Century Insurance Group, and other insurance-linked individuals or firms.
- Kansas Legislature SB 20 page says the Kansas consumer prescription protection and accountability act was signed by the governor and is associated with SB 360. SB 360's page says the original PBM bill died.
- KMUW/High Plains Public Radio reported the House passed the PBM measure 104-17 after SB 360 was inserted into SB 20; it also reported Hawkins had appeared set to keep SB 360 from moving.

### Taxes / Budget / Property Taxes

- Campaign site lists tax accomplishments: eliminating food sales tax, eliminating state income tax on Social Security, lowering state income tax, reducing the state portion of property tax, and a budget-process change the campaign says saved more than $210 million in the first year.
- Kansas Legislature HB 2043 page says the 2026 bill provided a protest petition to contest certain increases in property-tax revenues and was vetoed by the governor.
- Prior local harvest captured Hawkins' property-tax messaging through campaign/social/news amplification; direct live Facebook verification remains blocked/limited.

### Healthcare / Medicaid Expansion / Public Assistance

- Campaign site says Hawkins has led efforts for more than a decade to prevent expansion of Obamacare in Kansas.
- Prior local raw dump and social harvest include Kansas Reflector coverage of Hawkins speaking against Medicaid expansion and later budget-cut/Medicaid-cut framing. Use those as secondary context; do not treat them as official voting records.
- 2025 vote record includes Hawkins voting yea on SB 29 veto override, removing local health officers' authority to prohibit public gatherings for contagious-disease control.

### Abortion / Family / Gender Policy / Religious Liberty

- Campaign site says Hawkins is pro-life and says his leadership helped override vetoes on pro-life bills, protect girls' sports, and ban gender-transition surgeries on Kansas children.
- 2025 official vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on HB 2062 final action and veto consideration, a bill concerning child-support orders for unborn children.
- 2025 official vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on SB 63 final action and veto consideration, the Help Not Harm Act concerning gender-transition care for minors.
- 2025 official vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on HB 2311 final action, concurrence, and veto consideration, a foster-care/religious-or-moral-beliefs bill involving sexual orientation or gender identity.
- 2026 official roll-call page shows Hawkins in the yea column on SB 244 emergency final action, concerning sex-designated private spaces, gender definitions, driver's licenses, and birth certificates. The same measure also had a February 18, 2026 veto-override roll call listing Hawkins in the yea column in search results; the direct vote page should be retained for compile.

### Guns / Election Integrity / Immigration / COVID Authority

- Campaign site lists pro-Second Amendment measures and an NRA A rating, election-integrity veto overrides, ending COVID lockdowns, banning sanctuary cities, and provisions to help the Trump administration push back on illegal immigration.
- 2025 vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on HB 2052 final action/concurrence, a concealed-handgun license bill.
- 2025 vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on HB 2016 final action/concurrence, a voter-roll/election administration bill.
- 2025 vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on HB 2020 final action/concurrence, a bill requiring reports on certain noncitizens issued driver's licenses.
- 2025 vote record shows Hawkins voting yea on SB 29 final action/veto consideration, a bill limiting local health officers' authority over public gatherings.

### Governance / Legislative Leadership / Redistricting

- Kansas Legislature profile confirms Hawkins is Speaker of the House and chair/leader on several leadership-related committees.
- KCUR/The Beacon reported January 6, 2026 that Hawkins said redistricting was a priority but he lacked the votes to pass maps over a governor veto.
- Kansas Reflector/News From The States reported in November 2025 that Hawkins stripped at least three Republican colleagues of committee chairmanships after they declined to sign a special-session petition for congressional redistricting. Treat this as secondary-source governance context.
