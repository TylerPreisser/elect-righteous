## Evidence Packets

### 1. Current Role and Status

- Official staff directory: Wilson is listed as Superintendent and Administrator.
- HighBond members page: Wilson is listed as Superintendent among meeting participants, while board members are separately labeled.
- Interpretation: do not present Wilson as a board candidate. He is an appointed administrator accountable through the board.

### 2. Hiring and Background

- The Guidon reports the board approved Wilson after the Feb. 11, 2019 meeting, effective July 1, 2019.
- Hays Post's 2019 profile reports prior roles in Herington, Abilene, and North Ottawa County, plus Bethany/Kansas State education credentials.
- Hays Post profile theme: Wilson emphasized student-centered leadership, teacher relationships, facilities consensus-building, and positive culture.

### 3. Governance / Administrative Accountability

- January 2026 minutes: district goals include student achievement, staff, communication, and resource management.
- March 2026 contract coverage: Wilson argued against individual votes on administrators and defended the team. Park/Yarmer raised contract-language and individualized-review concerns.
- April 2023 nepotism coverage: board debated the district hiring Wilson's wife. Hays Post reported the policy required disclosure and did not specifically bar administrator relatives from district jobs.

### 4. Bond / Facilities / Capital Projects

- 2021 survey story: Wilson said survey results could signal readiness to discuss another bond and that facilities affect students' sense of community investment.
- 2022 bond story: board sent a $143.5 million bond to voters; Wilson framed it around space, CTE, auditorium, maintenance, and building capacity.
- 2025 project-manager conflict: Wilson defended Nabholz and administration as owner representatives; Park/Yarmer pushed for another board-facing project manager; motion failed 3-4.
- 2025 Wilson opinion: he argued critics should not lose sight of creating better future conditions for students/staff/community.
- 2025 ribbon cutting: Wilson publicly welcomed the community to the new high school and called it an investment in students.
- 2026 summer construction: Wilson outlined remaining projects beyond bond work, including Felten, O'Loughlin, ADA access, parking, HVAC, playground equipment, and technology rotation.

### 5. Bond Election Conduct

- April 2022 Hays Post: anonymous complaint after Wilson emailed staff that he would buy lunch/dessert for buildings with 100 percent eligible staff voting.
- Same article: email did not specify voting for the bond.
- May 2022 county attorney statement: no prosecution and no action. Treat as prosecutorial discretion/resource-priority/no-action record.

### 6. Mental Health / Student Supports / State Funding

- 2021 testimony: Wilson said 155 Hays High/Hays Middle students used MHIT in the 2020-21 school year.
- 2021 testimony: reported improvements in academic performance, attendance, internalizing symptoms, and externalizing symptoms for subsets of students.
- 2021 testimony: requested referral expansion, summer services, telemedicine access, pediatric integration, and workforce solutions.
- 2023 Hays Post: Wilson criticized proposed state legislation and private-school funding proposals, and discussed special-education underfunding.
- 2026 Citizen Journal: Wilson warned about possible mental-health funding cuts, special-ed funding shortfalls, and HB 2468/private-school tax credits.

### 7. Fees and Nutrition

- 2025 Hays Post: free breakfast continued; lunch and afterschool care increased. Wilson opposed a further fee reduction, citing textbook/classroom-material funds and new English curriculum needs.
- 2026 Hays Post: free breakfast continued; lunch increased five cents. Wilson said food cost increases made the adjustment necessary.

### 8. Safety, Bathrooms, Vandalism, Discipline

- 2023 Hays Post: private bathrooms at the new high school drew support and opposition over privacy, bullying, safety, monitoring, and design.
- 2026 Hays Post/Tiger: Wilson said bathroom monitoring all day is not realistic, cameras track entrances/exits, discipline occurs when responsible students are identified, and bathrooms would be cleaned twice daily.
- 2026 coverage: several board members asked for incident numbers/documentation; Wilson said maintenance-ticket review would likely be needed.
- Social angle: coverage described Facebook-driven community discussion, but no verified Wilson personal Facebook behavior was observed.

- Verified official district channel: USD 489 live feed, including posts attributed to Wilson.

- No verified current campaign/personal accounts with public follows, likes, comments, or posts found across major platforms in this pass.
