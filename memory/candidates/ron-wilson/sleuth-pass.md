## Executive Findings

- Ron Wilson is a current appointed administrator, not an elected candidate. The official USD 489 staff directory lists him as Superintendent, and the district meeting portal lists him as Superintendent in the board-meeting member roster. Any candidate page should frame him as the superintendent whose public record appears in board materials and local coverage, not as a campaign with ballot promises.
- No current Wilson campaign website, campaign committee, donor ledger, or campaign finance report was found. This is expected for an appointed superintendent. Existing candidate-style metadata should not infer a campaign from the USD 489 staff page.
- The strongest primary-source record is administrative: district staff directory, USD 489/HighBond meeting records, district news/live-feed posts, and Wilson's Kansas Legislature testimony on school mental-health services. Wilson's public record is mainly about facilities, district operations, state education policy, student supports, and board governance.
- The major correction from older Ron Wilson memory is source hygiene. The existing `site-profile.md` source list includes unrelated candidate/church/social sources from other profiles. The v2 file should use Ron-specific sources only.
- The 2022 bond-email episode needs a neutral formulation. Hays Post reported that Wilson offered lunch/dessert for 100 percent staff turnout, not a reward for voting yes on the bond. The county attorney later declined to pursue charges and said he would take no action; that is not the same as a court ruling or exoneration.
- Social presence is thin. A public LinkedIn preview was found, but no verified Wilson-controlled current campaign/personal accounts on Facebook, X/Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky, Threads, Truth Social, Gab, Gettr, Substack, Medium, Reddit, or newsletter platform were confirmed. District live-feed posts attributed to Wilson are official district communications, not personal social behavior.

## Primary-Source Findings

### Current Role

- USD 489 staff directory: Ron Wilson is listed as Superintendent, Administrator, with district phone extension.
- USD 489 HighBond/Diligent meeting portal: Wilson appears in the public meeting roster as Superintendent alongside the elected board members and board clerk.
- This should anchor metadata as `status: current_appointee_not_ballot_candidate`, `party: NP`, `incumbent: true`, `occupation: Superintendent, Hays USD 489`.

### District Priorities and Governance

- January 19, 2026 HighBond minutes include the district mission/vision/goal language: high student achievement, effective staff, positive communication, and effective management of resources.
- The same minutes show Wilson working through the elected board structure on legislative updates and student-support staffing. This supports the frame that Wilson recommends, reports, and administers; the board votes.

### Facilities and Bond Work

- USD 489 official news republished the 2021 Hays Post bond-survey story in which Wilson said survey results were an early step toward another bond discussion and framed facilities as part of community/student investment.
- USD 489 official news republished the 2022 story sending the $143.5 million bond to voters. Wilson publicly tied the bond to space needs, career/technical education, auditorium space, maintenance, and building capacity.
- March 24, 2025 HighBond minutes show a motion to look into hiring a separate project manager for remaining bond work failed 3-4. Hays Post gives context: Wilson defended Nabholz, third-party inspections, and administrative owner-representative work; board members Park and Yarmer raised oversight concerns.

### Mental Health and Student Supports

- Wilson's October 28, 2021 Kansas Legislature testimony is a strong primary source. He identified himself as Hays USD 489 superintendent, described bringing the Mental Health Intervention Team model to Hays, and reported 155 Hays Middle/High students used MHIT services in 2020-21.
- The testimony asked for more referral pathways, summer therapy/case management, telemedicine access, pediatric mental-health integration, and workforce solutions. It also uses Wilson's own language that youth mental health should be treated urgently.

### Official District Communications

- USD 489 live-feed posts attributed to Wilson in March 2020 show operational crisis communication during COVID closure planning, continuous learning, and free student meals. These are official district posts, not campaign communications.

## Secondary Coverage Findings

### Hiring and Background

- Hays Post's 2019 candidate profile reported Wilson's background: Herington superintendent, longtime Abilene Middle School principal, K-8 principal in North Ottawa County, Bethany College undergraduate degree, Kansas State master's/superintendent certification, and prior principal-of-the-year recognition.
- The Guidon reported the USD 489 board approved Wilson after a Feb. 11, 2019 board meeting, effective July 1, 2019.

### Bond and Facilities

- Hays Post's 2025 project-manager article is the best single source for the facilities oversight conflict: Wilson defended the current structure; Park/Yarmer wanted another board-facing manager; the motion failed 3-4.
- Wilson's March 2025 opinion letter in Hays Post is candidate-controlled/attributed language about the bond: he argued the district should keep building facilities students, staff, and community "deserve."
- Hays Post's August 2025 ribbon-cutting coverage shows Wilson as the public face of the new high school opening and names the project as funded through the $143.5 million bond.
- Hays Post's March 2026 summer projects story shows Wilson outlining additional capital projects beyond the bond work, including Felten/O'Loughlin work, ADA access, parking, playground equipment, HVAC, and technology/capital rotation.

### Election Conduct

- Hays Post's April 2022 story reported an investigation after Wilson sent a staff email offering lunch/dessert if a building reached 100 percent voter turnout. The same story notes the email did not specify a pro-bond vote.
- Hays Post's May 2022 follow-up published the Ellis County Attorney's statement declining to pursue election-bribery charges and taking no action. Treat as prosecutorial discretion/no action, not as a court finding.

### State Policy, Fees, and Student Supports

- Hays Post's February 2023 legislative story quotes Wilson criticizing proposals he said would divert money from public schools and noting special-education underfunding.
- Citizen Journal's March 2026 board-meeting recap reports Wilson warned the board about state mental-health funding, special education underfunding, and private-school tax-credit legislation.
- Hays Post's 2025 and 2026 fee stories show Wilson supporting free breakfast, food-cost-driven lunch increases, and resisting a further fee reduction because of curriculum/textbook/materials needs.

### Safety, Bathrooms, and Vandalism

- Hays Post's 2023 private-bathroom story documents the original controversy over the new Hays High bathroom design. Administrators and community members disputed safety, privacy, bullying, monitoring, and design implications.
- Hays Post and Tiger Media Network's April 2026 bathroom/vandalism stories show Wilson saying the district takes vandalism seriously, monitors cameras outside bathrooms, disciplines students when identified, cleans bathrooms twice daily, and cautions that Facebook claims may overstate the issue. Several board members asked for data/documentation; Wilson said maintenance tickets would likely need review.

### Administration and Personnel

- Hays Post's April 2023 nepotism story reports board debate after the district hired Wilson's wife as a teacher. The board approved personnel transactions, and the story says policy required disclosure but did not specifically prohibit administrators' relatives from district employment.
- Tiger Media Network and Citizen Journal's March 2026 administrator-contract coverage reports Wilson defended extending administrator contracts as a group while Park/Yarmer sought more individualized review/contract clarity.
