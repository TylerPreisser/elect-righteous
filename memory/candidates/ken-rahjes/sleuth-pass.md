## Issue-Mapped Observations

### Elections Administration and Voter Confidence

- The campaign homepage says Kansans want "fair and honest elections" and confidence that their vote is counted; the "Where I Stand" section says he supports more audits and work with county clerks/election officers and that he voted to end the three-day ballot-return grace period.  
  Source: https://kenforkansas.com/

- SB 4 is the primary official action anchor. The Kansas Legislature bill page identifies SB 4 as requiring return of advance voting ballots by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day and shows veto overridden. The official House journal and indexed roll-call records list Rahjes among House yes votes on final action and the veto override.  
  Sources: https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/measures/sb4/, https://kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/chamber/documents/daily_journal_house_20250325111406.pdf, https://kslegislature.gov/documents/view/?apn=b2025_26%2Fyear1%2Fready_for_publication%2Fjournal%2Fdaily_journal_house_20250227131205.odt

- Hays Post reported Rahjes saying voter education should begin in grade school, county clerks matter to election security, voter ID procedures should remain strict, and the Secretary of State should visit schools to teach election participation.  
  Source: https://hayspost.com/posts/d7c59a6a-6884-4ac9-aad1-7c9d728f5f5f

### Business Services and Economic Development

- The campaign filing post says Rahjes wants to streamline business filings and improve the One Stop online portal. The campaign homepage calls the Secretary of State's office an early stop for people starting or moving a business to Kansas and says the process should be expedited.  
  Sources: https://kenforkansas.com/news/rahjes-files-for-secretary-of-state/, https://kenforkansas.com/

- Hays Post reported Rahjes calling the Secretary of State's office the "front door" for operating in Kansas and connecting business growth to lowering property-tax pressure by growing the tax base. KVOE reported the same business-filings / entrepreneurs frame in Emporia coverage.  
  Sources: https://hayspost.com/posts/d7c59a6a-6884-4ac9-aad1-7c9d728f5f5f, https://kvoe.com/2026/03/16/rahjes-in-emporia-touting-secretary-of-state-bid/

- The campaign homepage says agriculture is the "heartbeat of Kansas," water must be used wisely, and Rahjes carried Water Task Force legislation.

Ken Rahjes, and identifies the bill as requested by Representative Rahjes. The House journal lists Rahjes among yeas on House final action.   Source: https://hayspost.com/posts/93f5e63a-dd1b-411a-98f8-c3ca8b2c187a
