Jason Hart is a Wichita Democrat running for U.S. Senate in Kansas. The most reliable current official anchors are FEC candidate ID `S6KS00304`, principal committee ID `C00942391`, Kansas SOS filing rules and election dates, and a Douglas County candidate-filing table showing Hart filed March 16, 2026 by fee for U.S. Senator with documents filed with the State of Kansas.
