## Official status check

Official Ellis County pages identify Bobbi L. Dreiling as the current County Clerk and County Clerk/Election Official. The county clerk page says the clerk is an elected four-year office and lists duties including county commission minutes, tax-roll certification, accounts payable, tax levies, and conducting Ellis County elections. The election page identifies Dreiling as the voter-facing election contact.

The official 2026 Primary Candidate Filings PDF lists Commission District 1, township clerk offices, Ellis City Council, and precinct committee offices. It does not list Ellis County Clerk as a 2026 office. That aligns with the county's split race structure: Dreiling belongs in `ellis-county-incumbents-off-cycle`, not `ellis-county-2026-ballot`.

The official 2024 results PDF on the Ellis County election-results page lists the county clerk race as Republican Bobbi L. Dreiling 10,211, Democrat Laura Allen 3,060, and 12 write-in votes. Hays Post's election-night article reported unofficial returns of 9,971 to 2,975; use the county official result for final vote totals and the Hays Post article only for election-night reaction.

## Observations (issue-mapped)

### Observation 1 - Current official footprint is administrative, not campaign-style

**What was observed:** Dreiling's strongest public footprint is official county infrastructure: clerk page, election page, staff directory, public election notices, agenda packets, and election-result documents. No current campaign website or active public social feed was verified.

**Issue mapping:** County clerk duties / election administration / property-tax administration. This belongs in `whoTheyAre`, methodology notes, and issue social signals only when tied to concrete official communications.

**Sources:**
- https://ellisco.net/93/Clerk - primary
- https://ellisco.net/425/Election - primary
- https://www.ellisco.net/directory.aspx?EID=19 - primary

### Observation 2 - Election-security messaging is detailed and procedural

**What was observed:** Dreiling's public remarks emphasize paper ballots, pre- and post-election testing, poll-worker training, post-election audits, matching ballot counts, election-security cameras, and a security/process manual. A county election-security-grant page links to state grant material. Hays Post reported Dreiling said a $6,000 election-security grant paid for a drop-box camera and contributed to cameras in ballot-counting and equipment rooms.

**Issue mapping:** Election security and election administration. The signal is procedural, not ideological.

**Sources:**
- https://ellisco.net/753/Election-Security-Grant - primary page linking state grant material
- https://hayspost.com/posts/64efa3e4-3296-4e5c-897f-8417a78e7fc3 - secondary/direct quote
- https://tigermedianet.com/?p=82423 - secondary/direct forum quotes
- https://www.ellisco.net/DocumentCenter/View/5809/public-test - primary public DS200 scanner demonstration notice

### Observation 3 - FHSU polling-location dispute is the main contested public issue

**What was observed:** Dreiling closed the 601 Main polling location near Fort Hays State University and combined precincts at Messiah Lutheran Church. Her stated reasons were low turnout, ADA compliance at another site, parking/navigation/logistics, cost, and serving the whole precinct rather than only students. Students and the ACLU argued for a Fort Hays State campus site, including a petition delivered in April 2024.

**Issue mapping:** Voting access / polling-location logistics. This is the clearest issue where public criticism and Dreiling's stated rationale need to be shown side by side.

### Observation 4 - Public access actions are mixed: Saturday early voting and notifications expanded, some drop boxes removed

**What was observed:** Dreiling said she implemented Saturday early voting and text/email election notifications. She also removed offsite ballot drop boxes in Ellis and Victoria, citing security procedures, bipartisan staffing/key-retrieval requirements, lack of observed usage in November 2023, and budget limits. The county election page gives advance-voting, mail-ballot, and VoterView access information.

**Issue mapping:** Voting access / election logistics. Keep the action evidence factual: expanded some access channels while limiting some physical drop-box locations.

**Sources:**
- https://ellisco.net/425/Election - primary
- https://hayspost.com/posts/64efa3e4-3296-4e5c-897f-8417a78e7fc3 - secondary/direct quote

### Observation 5 - Voter education and youth civic outreach are current public actions

**What was observed:** In February 2026, Dreiling placed an "I Voted" sticker contest before the county commission as an informational election-office item. Her packet letter invited grades 6-12 to submit nonpartisan designs and said she hoped the contest would inspire future voters and support civics lessons. In April 2026, she reported 60 total entries and selected three student designs.

**Issue mapping:** Civic education / voter outreach. This is a current official action and should be included because it is the newest primary-source Dreiling-authored material captured.

**Sources:**
- https://www.ellisco.net/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_02032026-726 - primary
- https://www.ellisco.net/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_04072026-741 - primary

### Observation 6 - Property-tax and revenue-neutral-rate work is part of her stated clerk record

**What was observed:** The official clerk page lists tax-roll certification, budgetary accounting/accounts payable, tax levies, and orders approved by the board among clerk responsibilities. Dreiling told Hays Post she learned and handled July and November abstracts, mill levy, and revenue-neutral-rate work before becoming clerk, and said she was the first Ellis County clerk to prepare revenue-neutral-rate mailings.

**Issue mapping:** Property tax administration / county records. This is not a general tax-policy stance; it is an administrative responsibility and claimed office accomplishment.

## Donor / funding sweep

No verified candidate donor ledger or complete Bobbi Dreiling campaign-finance report was found online in this pass. The Kansas Public Disclosure Commission says local candidates file appointment-of-treasurer forms with the county clerk/county election commissioner, and covered candidates file either an affidavit of exemption or receipts-and-expenditures reports. The statewide online contribution search is oriented to state races and did not surface a usable Bobbi Dreiling donor table.

Existing local memory mentions an ethics-filed county Republican committee expenditure trail with a $999 text broadcast for "Clerk Bobbi Dreiling" and a $50 meeting-room rental, but no retrievable source URL or PDF was found in this pass. Do not carry those amounts forward as confirmed donor/funding evidence unless the underlying report is recovered.
