# Max L. Dibble

## Who They Are
Max L. Dibble was added because House District 110 includes parts of Ellis County and the state audit found the race missing from the site. Public records identify Dibble as a Phillips County commissioner. Phillips County minutes show him participating in county votes and budget/operations discussions, and the City of Phillipsburg lists him as the Phillips County Commission District 2 representative on its Economic Development Advisory Board.
