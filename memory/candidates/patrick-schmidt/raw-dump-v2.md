### HB 2062 floor amendment

Kansas Legislature HB 2062 page lists a floor amendment offered by **Sen. Patrick Schmidt** on **2025-03-11**. The amendment would have amended K.S.A. 2024 Supp. 79-32,121 to add a personal exemption of **$2,320** for "any unborn child as defined in K.S.A. 23-3001," and required applying to the Department of Revenue for a taxpayer identification number for the unborn child. It also added title language: "providing for an income tax exemption for unborn children."

This is action evidence, but it must be paired with the roll-call record showing Schmidt voted **Nay** on final action/CCR/override for HB 2062.
