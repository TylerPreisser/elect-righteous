## Observations (Issue-Mapped)

### Issue: Property taxes and tax relief

- Schmidt sponsored **SB 90**, which would provide a property tax exemption from local government levies for the first **$100,000** of appraised value for certain owner-occupied homes and let local governments propose a ballot question to opt out. The bill is marked **Died**.  
  - Issue-mapping note: direct action evidence for property-tax relief.
  - Source: Kansas Legislature SB 90 - `https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/bills/SB90/`

- Schmidt sponsored **SB 201**, a homestead property tax exemption bill for certain veterans and former law enforcement officers with service-connected disabilities. The bill is marked **Died**.  
  - Issue-mapping note: direct action evidence for property-tax relief targeted to veterans/former law enforcement.
  - Source: Kansas Legislature SB 201 - `https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/bills/SB201/`

- Schmidt voted **Yea** on **SCR 1603** on **2025-02-06**, a proposed constitutional amendment to limit property tax valuation increases for real property and mobile homes. He also voted **Yea** on emergency final action for **HCR 5011** on **2025-03-21**, but later voted **Nay** on conference committee report adoption attempts on **2025-03-26** and **2025-03-27**.  
  - Issue-mapping note: direct roll-call evidence; use dates and motion labels to avoid flattening different versions/actions.
  - Source: Kansas Legislature 2025 vote record - `https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/vote-record/sen_schmidt_patrick_1/2025/`

- Kansas Reflector reported Schmidt saying, "I would love to see real property tax relief for Kansans," and noted he was one of two Democrats to vote for SCR 1603 in the Senate.  
  - Issue-mapping note: secondary source with candidate quote and voting context; useful for narrative, not a substitute for roll-call.
  - Source: Kansas Reflector - `https://kansasreflector.com/2025/03/03/freshman-kansas-senator-trying-to-stop-bad-stuff-while-pressing-for-property-tax-relief/`

### Issue: Healthcare, Medicaid, prescription drugs, and local health authority

- Schmidt's campaign health-care page says he supports access to affordable health care, expanding Medicaid in Kansas to extend care to **150,000** people statewide, lowering prescription drug costs, standing up to the pharmaceutical lobby, supporting legislation such as the Lower Drug Costs Now Act, and bringing more federally qualified health centers to Kansas.  
  - Issue-mapping note: candidate-controlled stated position for healthcare.
  - Source: campaign health-care page - `https://patrickforkansas.com/issue/affordable-health-care/`

- Schmidt voted **Nay** on **SB 29** final action substitute on **2025-02-19**, concurrence on **2025-03-20**, and veto override on **2025-04-10**. The measure removed authority of county or joint boards of health or local health officers to prohibit public gatherings when necessary for control of infectious or contagious disease.  
  - Issue-mapping note: action evidence for public health/local health authority; not directly Medicaid.
  - Source: Kansas Legislature 2025 vote record - `https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/vote-record/sen_schmidt_patrick_1/2025/`

### Issue: Labor, wages, paid leave, trade, and tax policy

  - Issue-mapping note: candidate-controlled stated position for labor/trade/tax policy.
  - Source: campaign labor page - `https://patrickforkansas.com/issue/unions-and-the-middle-class/`
