## Issue-Mapping Observations

### Education, school choice, and parental authority

- The campaign Education page is Eakins' most developed candidate-controlled issue page. It cites NAEP reading/math figures, says parents know best what children need, and lists proposed actions: school choice, civics education, reading/math outcomes, repeal of mandatory teacher-union negotiation, a new school-finance formula, school-board training, and teacher training focused on outcomes. Source: `https://www.kansascomeback.com/education`
- The same page says Eakins was the only Wichita school-board vote for school choice and against suing the state for more money, longer school days, and large superintendent raises. This is candidate-controlled record language; the specific roll calls were not fully reconstructed in this pass. Source: `https://www.kansascomeback.com/education`
- Kansas Legislature minutes for HB2466 say Joy Eakins, described as a small business owner who served on the Wichita school board and the Kansas State Department of Education CS task force, testified as a proponent of expanding computer-science education in Kansas schools. Source: `https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2021_22/measures/minutes/agenda_item_2022012646029990241`
- Hays Post's April 13, 2026 Ellis town-hall story reports Eakins saying she wanted a first-session education reform bill, school choice expansion, performance-based teacher pay, teacher protections, and classroom discipline changes. Source: `https://hayspost.com/posts/2c52043f-2180-461b-9a64-4fd75c91236f`

### Property taxes and local government spending

- The campaign Property Tax Reform page proposes appraisal caps at 3 percent or inflation, in-state comparables, regional assessment-dispute sessions, a burden-of-proof shift to government, fee reimbursement for successful appeals, and property-tax relief funds for families, seniors, and veterans. Source: `https://www.kansascomeback.com/propertytaxreform`
- Hays Post reported that Eakins' Ellis town hall focused on property-tax relief and education reform. It quoted her support for capping appraisal increases at 3 percent or inflation, whichever is lower, and reported her comments on appeals and local relief funds. Source: `https://hayspost.com/posts/2c52043f-2180-461b-9a64-4fd75c91236f`
- The Free State News debate transcript, an unofficial cleaned transcript, records Eakins describing rising appraisals as a backdoor tax increase and saying government should bear the burden of proof when appraisals increase. Source: `https://freestatenews.net/local-issues/transcript-of-the-gop-governors-debate-january-30-2026-part-1/`

### Business, economic development, and incentives

- The campaign About page says Eakins built Cornerstone Data, Inc. into a Kansas data-analytics firm with nationwide reach and served on the Wichita Board of Education. Source: `https://www.kansascomeback.com/about`
- The KAKE repost on the campaign site says Eakins framed the race around opportunity, young families leaving Kansas, education outcomes, a financial cliff, housing, and government getting out of the way of families and businesses. Source: `https://www.kansascomeback.com/news/joy-eakins-discusses-her-run-for-the-governors-office`
- The Free State News debate transcript records Eakins opposing special treatment for particular businesses and criticizing government-underwritten data centers and energy deals. Source: `https://freestatenews.net/local-issues/gop-governors-debate-transcript-part-2/`

Treat the transcript as secondary/unofficial, not an official transcript. Source: `https://freestatenews.net/local-issues/gop-governors-debate-transcript-part-2/`

Use this only as secondary context; the debate transcript is the more direct issue evidence.
