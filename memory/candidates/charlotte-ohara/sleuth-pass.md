## Issue-Mapping Notes

- **Taxes / spending / incentives:** Strongest issue. Campaign homepage, May 5 property-tax post, June 2023 Johnson County votes, CNANO tax-abatement no vote, and finance-themed posts map here.
- **Education / local control / DEI:** Campaign homepage calls for "Freedom to learn" and removal of DEI/WOKE/CRT; Apr. 28 post proposes closing the Kansas State Department of Education, ending federal funds, and localizing curriculum.
- **Election administration / voting / judicial selection:** Campaign homepage calls for direct partisan election of judges; March 24 post supports proof-of-citizenship/SAVE Act and paper ballots; 2024 legislative minutes show O'Hara testified for SB 366 on advance-ballot application mailings; May 19 post ties judicial-selection amendment to abortion and clergy outreach.
- **Abortion / life issues:** Feb. 17 poem and Jan. 14 abortion-statistics post are candidate-controlled anchors; May 19 judicial-selection post also frames the Kansas Supreme Court selection issue through abortion.
- **COVID / health freedom / public-health authority:** Biography and May 5 post say she refused a mask and shot and voted no on closing schools/businesses while a Johnson County commissioner; July 22 post supports the "health freedom movement" in the ivermectin debate.
- **Property rights / energy / China / industrial development:** Homepage opposes industrial wind/solar, lithium-ion battery manufacturing, battery storage, transmission lines, and eminent domain; CNANO posts and Johnson County records map to development incentives and China/security concerns.
- **Campaign finance / donor scrutiny:** O'Hara's own finance story is self-funding/loan-heavy. Her recent posts also critique opponents' donors; those are candidate claims and should be attributed to her unless independently verified.
