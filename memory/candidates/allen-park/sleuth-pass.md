## Issue Mapping Notes

### Governance, transparency, and board process

This is the strongest issue area because Park repeatedly states and acts on process concerns.

Evidence:
- In the 2019 KLC Journal survey, Park said USD 489 needed to rebuild trust, encourage involvement, increase communication, listen genuinely, and create an open culture that values multiple opinions.
- In the 2023 Hays Post Q&A, Park said communication should include minority opinions and board meetings should be inclusive and transparent; he also wrote that public board work "should not take place behind closed doors."
- In February 2023 coverage, Park argued board minutes should include more detail about reasons for no votes. Board counsel disagreed that Kansas law required that detail, and the coverage said the board's policy generally excluded summaries of comments unless the board voted to include them.
- In April 2023 nepotism coverage, Park said he was unsure whether USD 489 was following its policy and asked the board to look at the policy further.
- In January 2024 administrator-contract coverage, Park voted no on a one-year extension package and said the process was too fast.

Mapping reason: The public record connects his stated view of transparency to actions around minutes, personnel-policy review, and contract review. Keep the contrast factual: Park raised process objections; board counsel or other members sometimes disagreed.

### Bond, facilities, property, and taxpayer oversight

This is the second strongest issue area.

Evidence:
- In the 2022 USD 489 official bond article, Park asked about the cost of a special election, elementary-school size, school choice under realignment, and use of local contractors.
- In the 2023 Hays Post Q&A, Park said the board had responsibility to ensure bond work was executed correctly, timely, and fiscally responsibly.
- In March 2025 Hays Post coverage, Park supported looking into an additional project manager for remaining bond work, saying accountability to taxpayers was fiscally important. The motion failed 3-4, with Park, Derek Yarmer, and Curt Vajnar in favor.
- In September 2025 Hays Post coverage, Park voted no on a $69,480 asbestos-removal contract and against an $88,347 high-school change order. He said the no vote was about process and that he was not voting against doing the safety work.
- In November 2025 Tiger Media Network coverage, Park asked why the board was moving quickly on sale of 7.23 acres north of Hays Middle School, wanted an appraisal completed, and asked about water-rights value.

Mapping reason: Park's record does not read as simple opposition to facilities work; he supported bond completion in candidate materials while repeatedly pressing for documentation, process, appraisal, and oversight.

### Family impact, calendars, and student/staff supports

Evidence:
- In 2023 profile material, Park supported keeping class sizes manageable, reducing student fees where possible, technology needs, teacher training, and budget questions tied to academic achievement.
- In USD 489's February 2024 calendar article, Park asked whether the district was giving parents support on student days off and voted no with Derek Yarmer on calendars with monthly Monday professional-development days.

Mapping reason: This is narrower than governance/facilities but useful because it shows local operational concerns rather than state/national partisan positioning.

### Campaign finance and support

Evidence:
- In the 2023 Hays Post Q&A, Park said he had not received money outside the school district; the few donations he received came from family, friends, and community members who care about kids and education in Hays; additional expenses were paid personally.
- Kansas Public Disclosure Commission materials state the Commission administers the Campaign Finance Act for state offices and specific local offices: county, first class city, Wichita school board, and Kansas City Board of Public Utilities. Its blank form categories include state board of education and county office; city forms include first class cities, BPU, Wyandotte County Unified Government, and Wichita Board of Education. USD 489/Hays school board does not appear in those state-level categories.
- No donor-by-donor report, PAC ledger, or campaign committee filing for Park was found in the public sources checked.

Mapping reason: Include Park's own statement and the reporting limitation. Do not infer "no outside influence" beyond his statement.
